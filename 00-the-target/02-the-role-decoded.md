# 2. The role, decoded line by line

Job post: [GTM Engineer (Fresh grad, India) at Secfix](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/secfix/59670476-4f25-48a3-a11e-66c7a72416bf)

## In one sentence

They want a **builder who writes Python every day** to turn messy sales data and scattered tools into
**one reliable, automated system**, using **Claude**, and who cares whether salespeople actually
**close more deals**.

## The problem the role exists to solve

Today every salesperson runs their own setup for data, outreach and communication: their own
spreadsheets, their own lists, their own way of tracking. You replace that with **one central,
automated system**, and keep it reliable as the company grows.

## About the role

| They wrote | What it means | Skill behind it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Our first GTM Engineer… build the system our entire GTM team runs on" | Nobody has done this before; you design it from nothing | Systems thinking, prioritisation |
| "Replace that with one central, automated system" | Scattered spreadsheets and tools become one shared system | Pipelines, CRM, APIs |
| "Write code (Python, SQL) every day… not configure tools or run RevOps" | A real coding job, not clicking settings | Python, SQL |
| "Talk to everyone in GTM, spot the biggest bottlenecks" | You find the problem yourself | GTM vocabulary, listening, prioritising |
| "We own products and outcomes, not Jira tickets" | No one will hand you detailed tasks | Ownership |

## What you'll do: D-P-S-I-O

Memory trick: **"Don't Push Sales Into Overload."**

| Area | They wrote | Plain meaning | Where to learn it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **D**ata | "Make our CRM the single source of truth… model accounts, contacts and buying committees… pull data from multiple sources, deduplicate, score and write it back" | Clean, unique, scored companies and people in the CRM | [Python](../03-python), [pandas](../04-pandas), [data quality](../08-data-quality), [CRM](../07-crm-hubspot) |
| **P**ipeline generation | "Signal-based outbound, inbound scoring and routing, alerts that tell the right rep which account to call today and why" | Spot buying signals; score and route incoming leads fast; alert reps | [GTM foundations](../01-gtm-foundations), [webhooks](../06-apis-and-webhooks) |
| **S**ales automation | "AI tools and agents… from research to proposals to CRM updates" | Claude does repetitive thinking work for salespeople | [Claude and AI](../09-claude-and-ai) |
| **I**nfrastructure | "Central data layer, integrate tools via APIs and webhooks… scheduled jobs, error handling, monitoring and alerts when something breaks" | Connect tools; make everything run by itself and fail loudly | [APIs](../06-apis-and-webhooks), [production habits](../10-production-habits) |
| **O**wnership | "Investigate problems, find the root cause, ship the fix and document it so nothing depends on one person" | Debug, fix, write it down | [systems thinking](../12-systems-thinking) |

## How they measure success

> "Reps spend less time on manual work and more time with customers, and more pipeline comes from
> systems that run reliably and are actually used."

Two outcomes: **rep time saved** and **pipeline from reliable systems**. End every technical answer
by connecting it to one of them.

## Logistics from the post

- **Location:** remote, India.
- **Hours in sync:** 9:00–13:00 CET = **1:30–5:30 pm IST** in European winter (CET is UTC+1, IST is
  UTC+5:30, a 4.5-hour gap). In European summer (CEST, UTC+2) it becomes 12:30–4:30 pm IST.
- **Salary:** the post lists a range of 20k–32k USD.

## What a GTM Engineer is *not*

- Not RevOps (configuring the CRM by clicking settings, building reports).
- Not a sales rep (you don't sell; you build for the people who sell).
- Not a pure software engineer (you care about deals and reps, not just code).
- Not a data scientist (simple, explainable rules beat clever models here).
