# 1. Secfix: the company

> These facts were researched in November 2026 from Secfix's website, press and job post. Check
> [secfix.com](https://secfix.com) before your calls in case anything has changed.

## What Secfix sells, in one paragraph

Big customers (banks, car makers, large enterprises) will only buy software from a smaller company
if it can **prove it keeps data safe**. The proof is a **certificate** issued by an independent
auditor, such as ISO 27001, SOC 2 or TISAX. Getting one used to take 6–12 months of consultants and
spreadsheets. **Secfix sells the fastest way to get and keep those certificates:** software that
connects to a company's tools and automatically runs security checks and collects evidence, plus
human experts who guide the company through the audit.

**Analogy:** ISO 27001 is a board exam, the auditor is the examiner, and Secfix is the coaching
centre plus an automatic homework tracker.

## The product, piece by piece

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Compliance automation platform | Connects to AWS, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Jira, HR and device tools; runs 250+ automated security checks; collects audit evidence automatically |
| Human experts | Security and compliance experts guide each customer through the audit |
| CISO-as-a-Service | A part-time security chief for companies too small to hire one |
| AI features | CISO AI, AI questionnaire answering (security questionnaires from customers are a huge time sink) |

## The certificates and laws (what customers need)

| Name | Plain meaning | Who asks for it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ISO 27001** | International standard for running information security properly | European enterprise customers; the most common ask in Europe |
| **SOC 2** | US-originated security report | American customers |
| **TISAX** | Security assessment for the car industry | BMW, VW, Mercedes and their suppliers (big in Germany) |
| **GDPR** | EU law on personal data | Everyone handling EU personal data |
| **NIS2** | EU cybersecurity law covering many more sectors and companies than before | Companies in "essential" and "important" sectors and their suppliers |
| **DORA** | EU law on digital resilience in finance | Banks, insurers, fintechs and their tech suppliers |
| **ISO/IEC 42001** | Standard for managing AI responsibly | Companies preparing for the EU AI Act |

## Key facts

- **Founded:** 2021, Munich, Germany.
- **Size:** about 36 people, fully remote, working "in sync" in a virtual office (Gather).
- **Founders:** Fabiola Munguia (CEO, Forbes 30 Under 30, 2024), Grigory Emelianov (CTO, ex-Amazon
  and MAN, author of the remote-culture doc), Branko Džakula (CISO).
- **Funding:** $12M Series A (February 2026) led by Alstin Capital, with Bayern Kapital and neosfer
  (Commerzbank's investment arm), to expand across Europe and build more AI automation.
- **Customers:** European startups, SMEs and mid-market companies.
- **Competitors:** Vanta, Drata, Secureframe, Sprinto and others. Secfix's edge: European, with
  human experts built in.
- **Sales motion:** "Book a demo", no public pricing. That means **sales-led**: BDRs book demos, AEs
  run them and close.

## Why customers buy (the "why now" triggers)

These are the events that make a company need Secfix *now*. They matter because they are exactly
the **signals** your GTM systems will detect.

1. **A big customer asks for a certificate** in a deal ("we can't sign without ISO 27001").
2. **A new law applies to them** (NIS2, DORA) or to their customers.
3. **They raised money** and are now selling to bigger companies.
4. **They hire** a security, compliance or IT role, or a job ad mentions ISO 27001 / SOC 2.
5. **They expand** into the US (SOC 2) or into automotive supply chains (TISAX).
6. **A security questionnaire** keeps slowing their sales.

## The culture doc, and how it maps to GTM engineering

| Their principle | What it means for you as a GTM Engineer |
| --- | --- |
| Optimise for focus: light meetings, write things down | Document every system so nothing depends on one person |
| In sync first, but thoughtful: writing, short Looms, Gather | Explain your work clearly in writing and short videos |
| Product intuition, not feature votes; customer feedback is a signal, not instructions | Reps will ask for 20 automations; find the one bottleneck behind them |
| Care deeply about quality; zero high-severity bug policy | A silently failing pipeline loses leads, so build monitoring and alerts from day one |
| Small, temporary teams; people closest to the problem shape the solution | Sit with BDRs and AEs to see their problems first-hand |
| "All of it will be connected with agentic workflows" | Where your Claude experience fits |
